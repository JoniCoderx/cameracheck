    Troubleshooting & FAQ

    Is this test private?

    Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. Audio and video are never uploaded or stored on any server.

    My browser didn't ask for permission

    Click the camera/lock icon in the address bar and set Camera and Microphone to "Allow", then reload the page. On iPhone go to Settings → Safari → Camera/Microphone.

    The mic meter doesn't move

    Pick another microphone from the list, check that it isn't muted (hardware switch or OS settings), and make sure no other app (Zoom, Teams, Discord) is using it exclusively.

    Camera shows a black screen

    Close other apps that use the camera, check for a physical privacy shutter, try another resolution, or switch to a different camera from the list.

    Why is my FPS low?

    Low light makes many webcams lower their frame rate. Add light, lower the resolution, or plug the camera into a USB port directly.

    Does it work on phones?

    Yes — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and Samsung Internet on Android and iOS. Use "Switch camera" for front/back cameras.
